Gold Plan Coverage

Everything Included in Your AIRONE Gold Plan

The Gold Plan is AIRONE's most comprehensive maintenance option. It covers everything in the Silver Plan — the annual AC tune-up, furnace inspection, coil cleaning, filter check, and priority scheduling — and adds five features that make it the right choice for systems that run hard, homes in high-heat inland zones, and homeowners who want repair cost protection built into their coverage.

Foundation Coverage

Annual AC Tune-Up & Inspection

Your spring visit begins with a complete inspection of your AC system — condenser, air handler, refrigerant pressure, electrical connections, thermostat calibration, and all safety controls.

Annual Furnace / Heater Inspection

Your fall visit covers the full furnace inspection — burner operation, heat exchanger condition, igniter test, and safety shutoff verification before heating season begins.

Coil Cleaning — AC & Furnace

Both visits include coil cleaning. Clean coils maintain heat transfer efficiency and reduce compressor strain.

Filter Check & Replacement Recommendation

Your technician assesses filter condition at each visit and recommends the right replacement for your home's air quality conditions.

Priority Scheduling for Members

Gold members receive first-call priority scheduling — ahead of both non-members and Silver members during peak season demand.

Gold Exclusives

Bi-Annual Visits — Spring & Fall

Two professional visits per year: spring before the cooling season to prep your AC, and fall before heating season to inspect your furnace. Two visits means two condition reports and your system covered at both critical seasonal transitions.

Refrigerant Top-Off Included

If refrigerant is low during your spring visit, your technician tops it off at no additional charge. A standalone refrigerant recharge costs $200–$500. With Gold, it is included. Low refrigerant forces your compressor to work harder and shortens system life.

10% Off All Parts & Labor on Repairs

Any repair needed during your Gold membership year is discounted 10% on parts and labor. No minimum job size. No service category exclusion. On a $500 repair, that is $50 back. On a $1,500 repair, $150 back.

Emergency Priority Response

If your system fails outside a scheduled visit — mid-summer, a cold winter night, a weekend — Gold members are dispatched before non-members when emergency calls are triaged.

Annual Duct Inspection

Once per year your technician inspects accessible ductwork for disconnected runs, damaged insulation, unsealed joints, and airflow restrictions. The average California forced-air duct system leaks 30% of conditioned air. Catching duct issues early reduces energy waste, improves comfort, and reduces load on your HVAC equipment.

Spring & Fall Coverage

Two Visits Per Year — What Each One Covers

Gold Plan members receive two full visits per year, timed around Southern California's two peak demand seasons. Each visit follows the same licensed 8-point checklist. Each visit ends with a written condition report. Two visits per year means two sets of documentation — stronger warranty protection, and no developing issue going undetected for 12 months.

Visit 1: Spring (March–April) — AC & Cooling Prep

Scheduled before the Southern California cooling season begins. In high-heat inland cities — Burbank, Van Nuys, Glendale, Pasadena, Woodland Hills — cooling season can start as early as late April. Your spring visit ensures your system enters that period clean, calibrated, and with refrigerant levels confirmed.

  1. Inspect and clean evaporator coil
  2. Inspect and clean condenser coil
  3. Check and top off refrigerant if needed
  4. Test thermostat calibration and operation
  5. Test all electrical connections and safety controls
  6. Inspect blower motor and belt
  7. Clear condensate drain line
  8. Written condition report delivered at visit end

Visit 2: Fall (September–October) — Furnace & Heating Prep

Scheduled before heating season. Your fall visit inspects and prepares your furnace or heating system for winter demand and includes the annual duct inspection.

  1. Inspect furnace burner operation
  2. Inspect heat exchanger condition
  3. Test igniter and safety shutoffs
  4. Check filter and recommend replacement
  5. Inspect blower motor and belt
  6. Conduct annual duct inspection
  7. Clear condensate drain line
  8. Written condition report delivered at visit end

Southern California HVAC

Why Gold Earns Its Value Faster in Southern California

The Gold Plan pays for itself fastest in Southern California's high-heat inland cities. In Burbank, Van Nuys, Glendale, Woodland Hills, and Pasadena, summer temperatures regularly exceed 95°F and cooling demand can run from April through October. Systems in these zones accumulate more operating hours per year than coastal systems, wear components faster, and are statistically more likely to experience refrigerant loss and capacitor failure during peak demand. Two visits per year — one before the cooling season and one before heating season — means nothing goes 12 months undetected.

In Southern California's extended cooling seasons, even minor refrigerant loss that would go unnoticed in a milder climate creates real performance degradation. Your system runs longer to reach the setpoint, draws more power from your SCE circuit, and puts additional strain on the compressor. Gold's spring refrigerant top-off catches and corrects this at the beginning of the season — before the compressor absorbs a full summer of running low.

The average California forced-air duct system leaks 30% of conditioned air. In a Southern California home with a duct system running through an unconditioned attic — where summer attic temperatures can exceed 130°F — duct leakage means your system is fighting thermodynamics on two fronts simultaneously: cooling the air and losing it before it reaches the room. Gold's annual duct inspection catches the most common duct failure points before they compound into major energy loss.

Southern California wildfire season introduces a particulate load that accelerates filter degradation and coil fouling on a timeline that standard annual maintenance schedules are not designed for. Gold's fall visit catches post-wildfire-season coil and filter condition before the heating season begins — a check that is particularly valuable for homes in foothill communities, the Valley, and the Inland Empire.
